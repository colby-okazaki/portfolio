Lift App

A mobile-first workout tracker for logging lifts, building plans and tracking one-rep-max PRs. It installs to your home screen, works offline, and keeps all your data on your device.

Live demo →Code →

What it does

  • Workout logging. Log sets with weight and reps, see what you did last time, check sets off as you go, and drag exercises to reorder them. A rest timer runs between sets.
  • Plans. Build reusable workout templates from the exercise library, each exercise with its own sets and rest time, then start a workout from one.
  • PRs. Record one-rep maxes and chart your progress for each lift.
  • Exercise detail. History, a progress chart and an estimated one-rep max for every exercise.
  • Cardio. Track runs, walks and rides by time and distance.
  • History and streaks. Past workouts and your weekly streak on the home screen.
  • Settings. Pounds or kilograms, light and dark themes, and a default rest timer.

How it's built

  • No account, no back end. Everything is saved in IndexedDB in your browser, so nothing leaves your device.
  • Installable and offline. It's a PWA: add it to your home screen and it opens full-screen like a native app, with or without a connection.
  • Charts and drag-and-drop. Recharts draws the progress charts and dnd-kit handles reordering exercises.
  • A static site. With no server, it can be hosted anywhere. It runs on Vercel.

Stack

  • React
  • React Router
  • Vite
  • Tailwind CSS
  • IndexedDB
