Movie Ticket Booking

A cinema booking app for a fictional theater called Marquee. Browse showtimes, pick seats and manage tickets, with a separate admin portal. The lineup follows what's actually playing in US theaters and refreshes itself every day.

Live demo →Code →Demo login in the README

What it does

  • For moviegoers. Browse showtimes by date, pick seats on a live seat map, and cancel upcoming tickets.
  • For admins. Manage movies, showtimes and reservations, and see occupancy and revenue on a dashboard.
  • A real lineup. Titles come from TMDB's US now-playing and upcoming lists. Upcoming films get their first showtimes on opening day.
  • One theater clock. All showtimes are in Pacific Time, whatever the viewer's time zone.

How it's built

  • No double booking. Booking runs in a database transaction, so two people can't take the same seat.
  • A daily refresh. A scheduled job pulls the current lineup, fills a rolling window of about two weeks of showtimes, and removes week-old showtimes and titles that have left theaters, so the database stays a steady size.
  • Safe to run again. The refresh adds only what's missing and never touches real customers' reservations.
  • A schedule that looks real. Each title's number of screens is scored from its budget and popularity. Family films get matinees and horror runs late.
  • Believable demand. Seats are pre-sold to parties of 1 to 5 who sit together. Evenings, weekends and opening nights sell more.
